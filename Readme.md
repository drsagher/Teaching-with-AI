**Teach Smarter with AI: Prompts, Context and Classroom Content**

Teaching with AI is a specialized teacher training program designed to empower educators in Pakistan's public and private schools to harness the power of Artificial Intelligence as a teaching assistant. As classrooms grow more diverse and demands on teachers continue to rise, this program bridges the gap between emerging AI technology and everyday teaching practice. Participants will learn practical, hands-on skills in AI-assisted content generation, prompt engineering, context engineering, and other essential techniques to create engaging lesson plans, assessments, and learning materials more efficiently. Tailored specifically to the needs and realities of Pakistani school teachers, the program requires no prior technical background — only a willingness to learn. By the end of the training, teachers will be equipped to integrate AI thoughtfully and responsibly into their classrooms, saving time, enhancing creativity, and ultimately improving student learning outcomes across Pakistan.

**Audience:** Primary teachers (Grades 1–5), government and private schools
**Duration:** 6 contact hours, delivered as 3 sessions of 2 hours each. It can also run as a single day with breaks.
**Format:** 25% input, 75% hands-on. Teachers work in pairs on their own phones or laptops, using their own subject and grade.

By the end, teachers can:
1.	Choose an appropriate AI tool for text, images, voice or design tasks.
2.	Write effective prompts using a simple framework.
3.	Build a reusable "Context Pack" so AI outputs match their curriculum, class and locality.
4.	Generate a concept-based lesson and a project-based unit with worksheets, rubrics and activities.
5.	Check AI output for accuracy, cultural fit and age-appropriateness before using it.
